Frequently asked questions

Do you bring your own cleaning supplies?

Yes. Our team brings professional, eco-friendly products and all the equipment needed.

Are your products safe for children and pets?

Yes. We use non-toxic, eco-friendly products that are safe for the whole household.

How do I get a quote?

Fill out the estimate form or give us a call. We'll ask a few questions about your space and send a free, upfront quote.

Can I book a one-time clean or do I need a recurring plan?

Both work. We offer one-time, deep and move-in/move-out cleans, as well as weekly, bi-weekly and monthly plans.

What if I'm not happy with the cleaning?

Let us know within 24 hours and we'll come back to fix it at no extra charge.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No. Leave access instructions and our team will work independently.

Do you serve areas outside Calgary?

Yes, including Airdrie, Chestermere, Cochrane, Okotoks and other nearby communities. Just confirm your location when you book.
